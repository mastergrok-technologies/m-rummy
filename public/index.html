THE HOUSE RULES

A good hand starts here.

Learn the essentials before you play.

2–6 players. Two decks, one printed joker in each. Each player starts with 13 cards. The cut card decides the wild joker rank; a printed cut joker makes aces wild.

  1. Draw one, discard one. On your turn, take from the closed or open deck. Then discard one card. You cannot take an open joker or immediately return the card you took from the open deck.
  2. Find your sequences. A sequence is 3 or more consecutive cards of the same suit. A pure sequence has no substitute joker. A wild-rank card in its natural position can count as pure. Aces can be low or high, never wrap around.
  3. Complete your sets. A set is 3–4 cards of the same rank in different suits. Duplicate suits do not form a set. Jokers can fill missing cards in impure sequences or sets; all-joker groups do not count.
  4. Make your declaration. After drawing, select one final discard. Your other 13 cards must form valid groups with at least two sequences, including one pure sequence. The server checks everything. Rejected declarations leave your turn open, with no penalty.

Round penalties and profile points.

The winner scores 0 round penalty; lower penalty totals are better. A/K/Q/J count 10, numbered cards their value, jokers 0. Without a pure sequence all non-jokers count. With only one sequence, pure sequences alone are exempt. With two sequences and a pure one, all valid groups are exempt. Maximum loss: 80 points. First drop: 20; later drop: 40. You may drop out of turn, but must discard first if you have drawn.

Profiles start with 10,000 play points. Human wins earn play points separately; losses do not reduce your profile balance, and bot-only practice earns none. Each turn lasts 60 seconds. A missed turn is played automatically. Practice bots act on their own cards only. Refreshing the same browser reconnects you; keep its cookies.
